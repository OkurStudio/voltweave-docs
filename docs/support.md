# Support

## Where to get help

Support for Voltweave, and feedback on this documentation, is handled through **GitHub Issues** of the documentation repository:

**[github.com/OkurStudio/voltweave-docs/issues](https://github.com/OkurStudio/voltweave-docs/issues)**

Choose *New issue* and pick the form that fits: *Bug report*, *Question* or *Documentation problem*. Please look at [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) and the [FAQ](faq.md) first.

!!! danger "The issue tracker is public"
    Everyone on the internet can read what you post, and it can be indexed by search engines. Please **never** upload or paste into an issue:

    * your project files or assets (or links to them) that you do not want to be public,
    * license, purchase or order information, account names or keys of Fab, Epic Games or any other service,
    * personal data: your name, e-mail address, address, phone number or the names of other people,
    * the source code of the Voltweave plugin, which is a licensed commercial product.

    Before you paste a log, remove your Windows user name and other personal data from file paths. Describe your setup in words or with a few lines instead of attaching a project. If a problem seems to need private material, describe the problem without it first.

## What to include in a bug report

A good report lets us reproduce the problem quickly:

1. Unreal Engine version and the Voltweave version (see the plugin's description in **Edit > Plugins**).
2. Whether it happens in the editor, in Play In Editor, in a packaged game, on a server or on a client.
3. What you expected and what happened.
4. The output of **Build > Check Map** for your level.
5. The output of the console command `Voltweave.Dump` (see [Console commands](api/console.md)) and the relevant lines of the log (category `LogVoltweave`), with personal data removed.
6. The smallest setup that shows the problem: the nodes involved, their key properties and how they are wired.

## What this channel is

It is the support channel for now. Replies are written by the maintainers when they can; no response time is promised. A change of the support channel will be announced on this page and on the product page.
